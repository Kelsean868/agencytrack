Weekly WARs v2 — file your Weekly Activity Report, review your leaders'

WAR = Weekly Activity Report — the manager's edition of the agent weekly report: the regular production/activity numbers plus managerial KPIs (1-on-1s, joint field work, recruiting touches, training, unit meeting, dashboard review). Trevor (Branch Manager) files his own WAR up to the Sales Manager and reviews the 5 leaders who report to him (3 Unit Managers + 2 Supervisors) — that's the "5" on the nav. Targets are configurable by upper management (none mandatory yet), so every KPI reads gracefully with or without a minimum. Surfaces: My WAR · team matrix (filed / draft / not filed + 8-week consistency) · review drawer (approve / request changes). Desktop light + dark, plus mobile. Teal = ops · gold = filing streaks.
