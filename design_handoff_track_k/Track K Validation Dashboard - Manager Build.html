1
Roster — fan-out over new agents on financing
One row per agent with financingStatus = on_financing: status chip, term progress + miss count, suggested→confirmed, balance vs ceiling, adjustmentPct. Per-agent GETs, the Compliance-v2 fan-out — no collection query.
2
Override is the manager's final call
Suggested proration is advisory; managerFinancing is what posts. Caps at agreed (100%). The drawer shows the live adjustmentPct so the manager sees when a cut crosses the >10% notify line before saving.
3
Miss counter — confirmed basis ONLY
LOCKED (§2.4): the 0→3 counter advances only on settled-confirmed quarters. A provisional/at-risk quarter shows but never increments the counter — "pending settlement" until the BM confirms. No one is fired off a projection.
4
>10% flag = a duty, not a trigger
A confirmed downward adjustment past 10% raises the "notify Sales Admin by the 1st" obligation (5.3) in gold — a reporting step. It is explicitly not a termination; copy and color keep it a notification, not an alarm.
5
Surplus rows read as credit, not error
A negative running balance (P. Mohan, −TTD 3,100) renders in success green = "owed to agent", with the ceiling bar near empty. The balance machine permits credit — the roster states it plainly.
6
Notify rides the existing nudge primitive
"Notify Sales Admin" reuses the Compliance-v2 nudge transport (bell + email via the CF pattern). New recipient + template, established channel — not a new pipe.
