Foundation, no ledger dependency. Writes the per-agent financingTerms/{agentId} doc. agreedMonthlyFinancing drives the 6× ceiling; currentMonthlyFinancing is tracked separately (clause 5 can adjust it down); validatingAPI is the proration denominator; effectiveDate anchors the 24-month term, 12-month-service and first-3-months-waiver clocks (same anchor as contractDate). financingStatus is manager-set — not an agent checkbox — and gates all financing surfaces (§2.10). Four fields, two write distinct numbers
agreed ≠ current: agreed is the immutable ceiling driver (6×); current is the live, clause-5-adjustable draw. Keeping both lets a downward adjustment lower the draw without moving the ceiling. validatingAPI is the proration denominator; effectiveDate anchors every clock.
2
Derived, never entered
Ceiling = 6 × agreedMonthlyFinancing; the 24-month term, 12-month-service line and first-3-months waiver zone all compute off effectiveDate. Shown read-only so the manager sees the consequences of the figures they typed.
3
Status is manager-set & forward-only
LOCKED (§2.10): financingStatus is a manager control, not an agent checkbox. The machine advances forward; from on_financing the only direct move is → reconciling. cleared and post_financing_repayment are outcomes of the reconciliation event (Screen 6).
4
Status gates every other module
The dashboard hero, the proration engine, the take-home 50%-deduction and the garnish all read this enum. A mis-set status mis-renders the agent's whole picture — so the control is deliberate, audited (enteredBy / enteredAt), and confirmation-gated on save.
5
Form chrome reuses shipped primitives
Fields use the shipped .input / .label at ≥44px; the surface sits in the tenant-config tile pattern. Currency formatting via formatCurrency → TTD 8,000. No new field components.
6
Per-agent, not global — schedule history preserved
Terms are per-agent and mutable. A downward adjustment issues a new validation schedule; the monthly ledger stores the validatingAPI in effect that month so history never rewrites (§11).
⚑ Flags

One verify before the brief locks. Status transition guardrails — confirm the build enforces forward-only (no direct on_financing → cleared) and whether any backward move (e.g. reconciling → on_financing to correct a mis-set event) is permitted with an audit note. The four field semantics, the 6× ceiling derivation, and manager-set ownership are locked by §2 / §6, not flagged.
