Reconciliation event — total drawn vs total offsets, then settle.

The K6 event at end of year 1, or earlier on election to come off (6.5b). Tallies total drawn vs total offsets, applies the first-3-months waiver (only once 12 months' continuous service is met — earlier exit makes those 3 months repayable), and settles to one of two outcomes: OWING → post-financing garnish begins, or SURPLUS → balance cleared + lump sum paid to the agent.

Data canon

Writes the reconciliation record. financingReconciliation/{agentId}_{year}: totalFinancingDrawn, totalOffsets, waiverApplied, closingPosition, outcome ∈ owing | surplus, surplusPaid / garnishStarted. The waiver (6.6) applies only at 12 months' continuous service; an earlier exit makes the first 3 months' financing repayable. OWING → status post_financing_repayment + 6.2 garnish (10% commissions + 50% net bonuses + incentives) until cleared. SURPLUS → status cleared + lump sum. Worklist surface — no glass (census).

1
One worksheet, two triggers
The same drawn-vs-offsets tally runs at month-12 or on an early-exit election (6.5b). The event-bar toggle picks the trigger; the only thing it changes downstream is whether the waiver applies or reverses.
2
Waiver is service-gated, not automatic
LOCKED (§2.6): the first-3-months waiver (6.6) is forgiven only at 12 months' continuous service. Early exit reverses it — months 1–3 become repayable and are added back. The toggle makes the consequence explicit before the manager commits.
3
OWING → garnish, not a lump demand
LOCKED (§2.7): a closing debit moves status to post_financing_repayment and starts the 6.2 garnish — 10% commissions + 50% net bonuses + incentives, monthly until cleared. It is a repayment schedule, never a one-shot clawback.
4
SURPLUS → clear + lump sum
A closing credit (negative balance) moves status to cleared, closes financing at zero and pays the surplus to the agent as a single lump sum. The credit was a first-class value all along (Screens 1 + 4) — here it pays out.
5
Restyle of the reconciliation worklist
Mirrors the Policy Reconciliation surface: a worksheet → outcome → status-write pattern. New collection (financingReconciliation), same worklist chrome, same audit discipline.
6
Outcome drives the status machine forward
Reconciliation is the only path from reconciling to a terminal state. The two buttons are the two legal transitions — there is no direct manager move to cleared or post_financing_repayment (Screen 3).
⚑ Flags

Confirm before the brief locks. (1) Skipped-month gate — should an open reconciliation flag (Screen 4) block reconciliation until resolved, or run with a carried note? (2) Garnish on incentives — confirm "incentive payments" in 6.2 maps to the awards-engine payouts the app already tracks, so the 10%/50% withholding reaches them. The waiver-gating, the two outcomes and the forward-only status moves are locked by §2.6 / §2.7, not flagged.
