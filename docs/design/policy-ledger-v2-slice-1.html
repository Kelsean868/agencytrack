Slice rule

Presentational reorg only. Existing policy schema & keys, existing create form, existing status machine + transition fields, existing history subcollection — all unchanged. The only non-presentational requirements are the four locked corrections (derived Confirmed · state-machine-filtered transitions · Lapsed absent · neutral labels) and the three added states. Manager reconciliation, the Lens, and any awards coupling are out.
