Same component, role-gated & unit-scoped
This is the branch-manager FinancingRoster + ConsecutiveMissMonitor + DownwardAdjustFlag — filtered to unitId == thisUM and rendered in a read-only role. Not a rebuild — one fan-out, gated by role.
2
Override becomes a locked readout
The confirmed draw and adjustmentPct render with a lock and a "set by your Branch Manager" line. The UM sees exactly what posted and the distance-below-full — but the input is gone. No managerFinancing write path exists for this role.
3
The UM's two real levers: coach & escalate
Every actionable row offers Coach (note + read-only detail) and Flag to Branch Manager (escalation). These replace the BM's Confirm/Notify buttons — same rows, different verbs.
4
The >10% notify stays a BM duty — shown, not actionable
The 5.3 obligation is the Branch Manager's. On the UM surface it appears as status ("with Branch Manager"), not a Notify button — so the UM has awareness without owning a duty that isn't theirs.
5
Miss counter — still confirmed-basis only
Visibility changes, the rule doesn't: the 0→3 counter advances only on settled-confirmed quarters. The UM watches it climb and coaches, but no determination fires off a projection — and the UM can't confirm to make it count.
6
adjustmentPct: visible here, private on the self-view
The 5.3 trigger ratio is a manager-of-record figure — a UM sees it for their unit's agents. The same number is hidden from the agent's own self-view (K9). Role decides visibility; the field is the same.
