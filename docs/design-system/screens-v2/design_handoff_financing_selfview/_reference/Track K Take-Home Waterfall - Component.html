Component law

One source of truth, two outcomes. While owing (on financing OR post-financing repayment): takeHome = gross × (1 − taxRate − 0.50). Not on financing / debt cleared: takeHome = gross × (1 − taxRate). The 25% tax and the 50% split are configurable; the −50% posts to the monthly ledger as bonusOffset (K2). The component renders projected while the agent tracks toward a bonus and actual at payout — same chart, swapped inputs + a state tag.

The one-step calc
While owing takeHome = gross × (1 − taxRate − 0.50) Tax first, then 50% of the after-tax net (contract 6.1, locked §2.1). The 50% is taken unconditionally while on financing — no monthly cap; truing-up happens at the reconciliation event (§2.5). taxRate + the 0.50 split are config.

1
Net-first, then 50% — the locked order
LOCKED (§2.1): tax comes off first, then the 50% applies to the after-tax net (contract 6.1, "50% of net bonuses"). Never 50% of gross. The chart's two deduction bars are sized to that order so the math is visually self-evident.
2
Show projected, not just at payout
The whole point is pre-disappointment: render the projected take-home while the agent tracks toward a bonus on the dashboard — so the TTD 3,750 reality lands long before the payout statement does.
3
Two outcomes, one component
The financing column collapses to zero when financingStatus ∈ {not_on_financing, cleared}. Same chart, the −50% step simply isn't drawn — and the take-home bar turns success green to signal "you keep it all".
4
Posts to the ledger
The −50% (TTD 3,750) is the bonusOffset the monthly statement records (Screen 4 / K2). The waterfall is the human-readable face of that ledger line — projected here, reconciled there.
5
Basis badge on the actual state
In actual mode the header carries the shared basis badge: Settled — confirmed once the quarter settles, provisional while still tracking. Same three-state badge as every Track-K surface.
6
Repaying counts as owing
The "while owing" column applies in both on_financing and post_financing_repayment — the 6.2 garnish keeps the 50%-of-net rule running until the balance clears. Status, not month, decides which column shows.
⚑ Note

No open flags on the calc itself — the net-first order and the 50%-of-net split are locked (§2.1, §2.5). The only configurable risk is the numbers: taxRate and the gate/threshold values are 2017-vintage ruleset placeholders (§11). The component reads them from config live, so a current-year tax rate flows through without a code change. Both light + dark via Nexus tokens.
