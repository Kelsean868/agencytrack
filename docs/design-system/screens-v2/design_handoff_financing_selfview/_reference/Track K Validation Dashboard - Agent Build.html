Data canon

New track, flat schema. Reads financingTerms/{agentId} (status, ceiling) + financing/{agentId}_{YYYY_MM} (monthly ledger) + the contract API chain off the Policy Ledger. The bonus gate ($37,500), tax rate (25%), ceiling multiplier (6×) and persistency line (95%) are configurable placeholders from the ruleset. Real-time figures are provisional; determinations wait on the manager-confirmed basis (§2.4). Both light + dark via Nexus tokens; hero uses the Nexus Glass tier (one per screen).

1
Hero — Nexus Glass tier, balance vs ceiling
The one glass hero per screen (recipe census ✓). The ceiling track makes the 6× headroom glanceable and renders the zero line mid-track so a negative balance reads as surplus, left of zero — not an error. Status badge is the §5 state-machine value.
2
Quarterly tracker — two gates, one chip
Gross-vs-37,500 + persistency-vs-95% render as tick-marked tracks (same grammar as Persistency v2's two-tick band). Status chip ∈ On track / At risk / Qualified / Missed. Qualified only resolves on the confirmed basis — never off the live projection.
3
Monthly financing — proration made legible
actual ÷ validating = proration %, applied to agreedMonthlyFinancing → suggested draw. The basis badge sits in the header; month 7 reads provisional with the "trues up to settled" note. Manager confirms the final number on their surface.
4
Take-home waterfall — kills the "I thought it was TTD 10K" moment
Gross → −25% tax → Net → −50% → Take-home, shown projected while tracking (not just at payout). The gold footer states the off-financing comparison. The −50% posts to the ledger as bonusOffset (K4).
5
Year-end reconciliation projection
"At current pace: TTD X surplus / TTD Y owed" — the waiver-adjusted closing position, forward-looking. Sets the expectation for the reconciliation event (Screen 6) before month 12 lands.
6
Period drill-down — a slice of the live ledger
"Applications this period" is a period-scoped slice of the Policy Ledger list (Track H surface), tagged settled / submitted / carried-forward per §7. Reuse, not rebuild — requires usesPolicyLedger:true; else falls back to settlements + submissions with no per-policy drill.
⚑ Flags

Two open items touch this surface. (1) Quarterly bonus base — Net-for-Production vs Net-for-Persistency (§10.2; working interp = Production, used here). (2) Persistency formula — app's validated figure vs clause 1.6 literal (§10.1, flagged for veto). Both numbers are drawn against the working interpretation and labelled so the build swaps the resolved one. Every threshold shown (37,500 · 95% · 6× · 25%) is a ruleset value, not a constant — confirm current-year figures before seeding (§11).
