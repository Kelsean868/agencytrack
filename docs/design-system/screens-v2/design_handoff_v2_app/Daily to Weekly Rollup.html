The challenge

Everything in the weekly has to be capturable in a daily log that's still fast. The weekly report has four phases — Activity / prospecting, Sales, Reflection, Goals. Two of them (self-eval, next-week goals) are inherently weekly and are done once at close in every option. The real question is the prospecting + sales fields: which get captured day-by-day, and which become a Sunday backfill.

i

Self-eval and next-week goals are weekly in every option — rating a single day 1–10 or setting "next week's" goal mid-week is meaningless, so those two always wait for the Sunday review. They are not the gap. The gap is the five prospecting channels (★): A and B capture them day-by-day so the weekly is accurate; C leaves them to a memory-based Sunday backfill, which is exactly the prospecting data you said matters most.

The answer to your challenge

Capture all of it daily — but make the prospecting channels a single fast card.

The way to put the whole weekly into an easy daily log isn't to drop fields (C) or to make people step through a wizard (A) — it's to group the prospecting channels into one compact card of ± steppers that defaults every count to 0. A day with no seminars is one glance, no taps; a day of 12 dials is two taps. That's Option B: the daily stays a single screen, every prospecting activity lands in the weekly automatically, and only the genuinely-weekly reflection waits for Sunday.
