1
Pace row — NEW grammar over the S2 plan
Each committed metric becomes a track with floor tick (neutral), plan cap (teal, the row end), actual fill (success ahead/on-track · warning behind), and a pace marker. Reuses the S1/S2 chip + provenance idioms; no new tokens.
2
Variance is measured against PACE
"On track" = actual ≥ plan × elapsed ÷ total, so mid-week behind/ahead is honest, not judged against the full-week number on a Tuesday. The pace marker is the visible "should-be-here-today" line.
A
Actuals are READ — nothing created
Mid-week actuals come from Daily Capture (provenance chip "mid-week"); once the weekly report is submitted they switch to the submission ("final"). Variance is computed client-side. No store, no write.
3
Calls & Contacts — no daily source (honest)
Daily Capture has no dials/calls or prospect-contacts field, so these two rows show "weekly only · no daily pace" mid-week (hatched track) and only resolve to an actual at submission. Not invented. See the mapping flags.

The "behind" threshold
Is behind actual < pace exactly, or with a grace buffer (e.g. < 90% of pace) so early-week dips don't alarm? Operator's call.
?
Elapsed basis
Does "elapsed ÷ total" count calendar days (Sun–Sat) or working days (Mon–Fri)? Changes every pace marker.
?
Early-week suppression
Suppress variance states entirely on Day 1 (everything would read "behind" before any logging)? When does pace judgment "switch on"?
?
Floor below plan vs floor above plan
When a metric's floor exceeds the agent's plan (e.g. CI floor 10 > plan 6), which is the "real" target the card should hold them to? Doctrine says show both — but which drives the variance color?
