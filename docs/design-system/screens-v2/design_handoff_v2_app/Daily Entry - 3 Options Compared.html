The core tension, stated plainly: the daily was scoped lean on purpose because daily-logging speed drives compliance, and compliance is what feeds the weekly at all. A maximizes sameness at the most cost to that speed; C protects speed completely but leaves the dials/calls coverage gap; B is the middle path — it closes the field gap while keeping one screen, at the cost of a longer scroll and some schema work. They're also combinable: C's restyle is a low-risk foundation you could ship first, then layer B's fields on top. Tell me your pick and we'll lock the detailed mechanics.
