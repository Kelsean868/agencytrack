Daily breakdown · my call
Hold the daily breakdown for Slice 3 — not here.

A daily split is only meaningful against daily actuals (a Mon–Sun pace curve). In Slice 1 there's no actual to pace against, so a daily breakdown would either be a flat "÷5" restatement of the weekly number (noise) or imply tracking that doesn't exist yet (dishonest). Keep Slice 1 to the weekly suggested line; introduce the daily curve in Slice 3 when plan-vs-actual arrives. A single honest "≈ 10 dials/day" sub-label is the most it should hint, if anything.
