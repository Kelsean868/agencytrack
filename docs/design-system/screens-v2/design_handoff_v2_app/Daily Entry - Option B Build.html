PActivity points — anchored, in the cardNew
Each logged activity earns points; the running total sits in the Save / Submit card with a quick +N pulse on each tap — not a floating badge that fights the inputs on a phone. A bare number is vanity, so it shows pts vs daily pace (pace = your weekly activity-point floor ÷ working days). W6- or 7-day weeksNew
The week strip up top shows all seven days. Working days are a setting (M–F / 6-day / 7-day, top-right of the strip — tap it) and that denominator drives pace, the daily points target, and streaks. Days outside the schedule render dimmed and never break a streak or fire a “you didn't log” nudge — an agent can still log them, and they roll up the same way.
BBack-fill a dayNew
Tap any day in the strip to fill or edit it. Logged days (green ✓) reopen for edits; a missing weekday shows amber as the catch-up cue. Editing a past day re-syncs into the same WK 48 draft via the aggregator. Only the current open week is editable — once submitted/committed it's read-only. Try tapping Mon on the phone.
