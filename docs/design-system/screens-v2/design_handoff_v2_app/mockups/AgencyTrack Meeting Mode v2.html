Meeting Mode v2 — the guided stand-up

The full-screen "Start meeting" stand-up, rebuilt to the v2 grammar as a guided presentation rather than a stat slideshow. It opens on the branch's reality, then gives the room the bird's-eye — branch scorecard (this week · month · quarter · year), units team-by-team, and two master sheets (everyone's activity + the printed production report). It drills exception-first through each agent's week against the company's minimum weekly activity floors, lands recognition (top producers + most-active + an "on the rise" strip), celebrates the week's birthdays & anniversaries, points at awards within reach, and wraps on actions captured. A 1-on-1 step adds coaching ratios + self-evaluation. Open Tweaks to flip the whole deck light/dark (dark default), hide presenter chrome, or set the activity sheet's group/sort default. Drag in your own team photos (they persist, separate from kiosk). Plus a phone presenter remote. Use ←/→ to walk the run.
