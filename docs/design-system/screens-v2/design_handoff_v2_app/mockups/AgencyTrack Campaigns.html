Campaigns v2 — time-boxed pushes, qualify ladders & the persistency gate

The new Campaigns surface, built to the v2 grammar. A campaign is a time-boxed push toward a target, scoped to where it was created — a Branch Manager runs branch/unit/individual ones; agency-wide stays with the Sales Manager. Two structures: qualify-target ladders (hit a tier, win its prize — the company default, modelled on the real Christmas Campaign 2025) and 1st/2nd/3rd placement races. The signature mechanic is the persistency gate — every payout scales by quality (≥90% full · 85–89% half · 80–84% quarter · <80% disqualified). Manager surfaces: index · live standings · builder · confirm-winners. Agent surfaces (mobile): my prize locked, gate health, reach for the next tier. Teal = operations · gold = the prize layer.
