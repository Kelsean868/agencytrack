Light theme is the constraint
At airier-light the muted tier sits right on 4.5 — so airier light brightens muted to #E4F1F1 to stay legal, and can't go below α .86 without failing. If you want maximum glass, it lives in dark theme.
✓
Dark theme has room to breathe
The dark backdrop keeps the effective floor deep even at α .70, so dark airier still clears AA comfortably with the same inks — this is where the see-through glass really shows.
