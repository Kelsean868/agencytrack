CRO Sign-on v2 — the branch's operational hub & the ledger's point of entry

The Customer Relationship Officer gets her own sign-on. She's the human at the source of truth: she receives printed policies from head office, notifies agents, and tracks the 30-day clawback clock (commission is withheld if a policy isn't delivered within 30 days of the head-office dispatch date — a client can also decline in that window: Not Taken). She data-enters every policy on Tatil, issues & settles the two branch products (Annuities + Funeral Expense) while recording returned issue/settlement dates for the rest, and compiles the Friday 4pm production report that feeds Monday's meeting. Five desktop surfaces + CRO mobile + the agent's own "policies to deliver" view (with the same clocks). Delivery confirmation writes the settlement date back to the ledger — feeding reconciliation, persistency, and campaigns. Teal = pipeline · amber = at risk · red = clawed/overdue · gold = branch-issued.
