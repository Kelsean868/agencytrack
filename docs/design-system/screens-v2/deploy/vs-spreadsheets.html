To be fair

What spreadsheets still do well.

Spreadsheets are free, familiar, and infinitely flexible — and for a two-person unit they may genuinely be enough. The workflow breaks at scale: the moment numbers cross a desk (agent to unit, unit to branch, branch to carrier), every hand-off adds delay, re-keying, and doubt. AgencyTrack's case isn't that spreadsheets are bad software; it's that an agency's performance loop shouldn't depend on hand-offs at all.
