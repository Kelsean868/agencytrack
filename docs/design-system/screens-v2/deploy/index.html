The trust gate

Every number traces to one reconciled ledger.

  • Agents log it once. Production, persistency, awards, and campaign payouts all read the same data — never re-keyed.
  • The CRO confirms it. Each settlement is matched to head office; mismatches, NTUs, duplicates, and partials surface as exceptions.
  • Delivery protects pay. A 30-day clawback clock chases every policy to a signed receipt before commission is withheld.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is AgencyTrack?

AgencyTrack is a performance and agency-management platform for life-insurance agencies across the Caribbean, built in Trinidad & Tobago. It tracks agent activity, reconciles commissions against carrier settlements, and runs goals, awards and recognition — without being a CRM.

Is AgencyTrack a CRM?

No — deliberately. AgencyTrack manages the agency's performance loop: planning, activity logging, coaching, settlement reconciliation, and recognition. Agents keep their client books wherever they already work.

How does commission reconciliation work?

Agents log business once. The back office reconciles each entry against the carrier's settlement file, flagging exceptions like status conflicts and unmatched entries. A 30-day delivery clock tracks policies at risk of clawback, so commission is protected before it's withheld.

Who is AgencyTrack for?

Every seat in the agency: agents, unit managers, branch managers, sales managers, the CRO, and the back office. Each role gets its own dashboard and sees only what its role permits.

Where does AgencyTrack operate?

AgencyTrack is built in Trinidad & Tobago and designed for life-insurance agencies across the Caribbean. The platform currently runs natively in TTD — production figures, goals, campaign thresholds, and settlement reconciliation.

Are awards and campaigns based on persistency?

Yes. Persistency is gated into every campaign and award, so recognition pays out on quality business that stays on the books — not on churn.
