1 · CRO — the surface

The Customer Relationship Officer is the branch's operational hub and the human at the ledger's point of entry (Anessa Baptiste, South Branch in the mockup). She receives printed policies from head office, tracks collection + delivery against the clawback clock, data-enters every policy into Tatil, issues + settles the two branch products, and compiles the Friday 4pm production report.

The mechanic — the 30-day clawback clock

Commission is withheld and clawed back if a policy isn't delivered within 30 days of the date head office dispatched it (the sent date). A client may also decline in that window (Not Taken). daysLeft = 30 − daysOut; at-risk ≤ 7 days left; overdue < 0. Color language: teal · pipeline amber · at-risk red · clawed/overdue gold · branch-issued.

4 · Data model & state machine

Delivery lifecycle

Received→ Notified→ Collected→ Out→ Delivered→ Receipt in exits: Not Taken Undeliverable Clawed

open states (Received → Out) count against the clock; terminal states close it. Clawed is the automatic terminal when an open policy passes 30 days.

Firestore shape (suggested)

tenants/{tid}/deliveries/{deliveryId}
{
  policyNo, owner, product, plan, api,          // policy identity
  agentId, agentName, unitId, branchId,        // scoping (branchId = auth clamp)
  sentDate,        // head-office dispatch — DRIVES the clock
  receivedDate, notifiedAt, collectedAt, outAt, deliveredAt, receiptInAt,
  state,           // received | notified | collected | out | delivered | returned_receipt | not_taken | undeliverable | clawed
  settlementDate   // written back to policies/ ledger on delivery
}

Derived, never stored: daysOut, daysLeft, atRisk, overdue — compute from sentDate vs now (mirror deliveryClock() in the mockup). Constants: CLAWBACK_DAYS = 30, AT_RISK_DAYS = 7, BRANCH_ISSUED = ['Annuity','Funeral Expense'].

Ledger write-back (the integration)

Delivery confirmation writes settlementDate back to the policy in the policies/ ledger — which is what feeds reconciliation, persistency, and campaigns. Keep the write in a src/services/* service (e.g. deliveryService.js), never a direct component query, passing tenantId. Branch-issued products (Annuity, Funeral Expense) also get issued + settled here.
