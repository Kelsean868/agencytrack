The trust gate

Every number traces to one reconciled ledger.

  • Agents log it once. Production, persistency, awards, and campaign payouts all read the same data — never re-keyed.
  • The CRO confirms it. Each settlement is matched to head office; mismatches, NTUs, duplicates, and partials surface as exceptions.
  • Delivery protects pay. A 30-day clawback clock chases every policy to a signed receipt before commission is withheld.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is AgencyTrack a CRM?

No — deliberately. AgencyTrack manages the agency's performance loop: planning, activity logging, coaching, settlement reconciliation, and recognition. Agents keep their client books wherever they already work.

How does commission reconciliation work?

Agents log business once. The back office reconciles each entry against the carrier's settlement file, flagging exceptions like status conflicts and unmatched entries. A 30-day delivery clock tracks policies at risk of clawback.

Where does AgencyTrack operate?

AgencyTrack is built in Trinidad & Tobago and designed for life-insurance agencies across the Caribbean. The platform currently runs natively in TTD.
